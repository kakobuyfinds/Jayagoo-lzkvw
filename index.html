Jayagoo spreadsheet guide

A useful list starts with useful details.

A screenshot captures a look. A well-organized shortlist captures the information needed to make a decision. Give each item a source link, measurable attributes and a date so you can check it again later.

Find your starting point ↗

  1. Choose one category

    Start with the item you need most. Compare shoes with shoes and jackets with jackets before building a full outfit.

  2. Save the original listing

    Record the product URL, seller, selected color and size. Similar-looking photos can represent different items.

  3. Compare fit and total cost

    Use measurements alongside size labels. Record item price, currency and available shipping estimates separately.

  4. Recheck before ordering

    Open the current listing to confirm stock, specifications, delivery restrictions and return terms.

What belongs in your spreadsheet?

A simple framework you can copy into your own sheet

Recommended columns for comparing fashion finds
Field What to record Why it helps
Product source Original URL, seller name and date checked Makes the item easier to revisit and verify.
Category & variant Item type, color and selected option Keeps different variants from being mixed together.
Fit Size label, measurements and measurement units Supports comparison with a similar item you already own.
Materials Listed fabric, composition, finish and care instructions Helps evaluate suitability and maintenance needs.
Cost Item price, currency, shipping estimate and any known fees Separates the advertised price from the potential total cost.
Open questions Missing specifications, seller questions and return conditions Highlights information to confirm before purchasing.

Clear answers

Before you start browsing.

Understand what this guide provides, where its links lead and which details still need checking at the source.

What does Jayagoo spreadsheet mean on this page?

This page uses Jayagoo spreadsheet as a search topic for organizing and comparing fashion finds. It provides category shortcuts and a comparison guide, rather than an official Jayagoo spreadsheet or service.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

No downloadable spreadsheet is provided here. You can create your own shortlist using columns for product URL, category, size, material, item price, shipping estimate and the date checked.

Where do the shopping links go?

General shopping links open www.cafans.com. Category shortcuts open the corresponding category pages on cafans.com.

Are brand references proof of authenticity or availability?

No. Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès are referenced as fashion search interests. Brand references and editorial images do not verify authenticity, authorization, inventory or current availability.

What should I check before purchasing?

Check the current product listing, seller details, measurements, materials, total cost, shipping restrictions and return terms. Do not rely on a saved spreadsheet entry alone.
